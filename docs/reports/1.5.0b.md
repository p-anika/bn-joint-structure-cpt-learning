## Surprises

1. **Jirka's files have Windows (CRLF) line endings**: `BN-data.csv`, `preprocessed-data.csv`,
   `data24-training-1.dat`, `scoring2a.c`, `gobnilp.set`. `setup.md` wrongly said `BN-data.csv`
   was LF (corrected with a dated note). No number is affected (pandas/R handle CRLF; scoring2a
   reads our LF `.dat`), but it is why `.gitattributes` exempts the originals.
2. **The 44-variable DAG/CPDAG files existed before**: committed as `src/adjmat.*` in `fd2b479`
   and removed in `c97da60`. Regenerated now, they are byte-identical to those Sep 6 copies, so the files in `results/phase1/` are the same ones that briefly existed then.
3. Two open items from 1.5.0a are closed: the `gobnilp.set` warning goes to stderr (so `tee`
   doesn't save it), and SCIP's static libraries were built by GOBNILP's own `make`
   (`make -C scip libs`), now in `setup.md` §7b.
